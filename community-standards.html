Community Standards

Compete with skill. Win with honour. Respect every opponent.

Our Promise

Respect Every Player. Play With Integrity. IGEE ARENA is built for competition, sportsmanship and respect. Every player deserves to enjoy a fair and welcoming gaming environment.

1. Respect Every Player

Treat opponents, teammates, staff and other community members with dignity. Winning does not give anyone the right to disrespect another player. Losing is not an excuse for abuse.

2. No Harassment or Threats

Abusive language, harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination and targeted bullying are not acceptable.

3. Compete Fairly

Cheating, exploiting game bugs, using unauthorized assistance, manipulating match outcomes and deliberately misleading opponents are prohibited.

4. Honest Matches and Results

Submit truthful match results and genuine evidence. Do not impersonate another player, falsify screenshots or manipulate disputes.

5. Protect Your Account

Keep your password and verification codes private. Do not impersonate other players or attempt to access another person's account.

6. Report Misconduct

If you experience or witness misconduct, use the Report a Player feature. Submit accurate information and relevant evidence. Do not submit knowingly false or malicious reports.

Report a Player →

7. Consequences of Violations

Depending on the seriousness and circumstances of a violation, IGEE ARENA may take action including:

  • Official warnings.
  • Temporary match restrictions.
  • Temporary account suspension or freezing.
  • Review of reported matches and evidence.
  • Permanent account removal for serious or repeated violations.

Players may use the available appeal process to challenge an account restriction.

Our Final Message

Compete with skill. Win with honour. Respect every opponent. Together, we make IGEE ARENA a better place to play.
