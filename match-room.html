Five-minute response rule
Once a player submits an I WON claim with screenshot evidence, the opponent will have five minutes to respond. If the opponent confirms the loss, the evidence can proceed through verification. If the opponent also claims victory, the match goes to admin review. If the opponent does not respond within five minutes, the winning claim may qualify for automatic wallet settlement after the required evidence and security checks.
Important: If neither player submits a winning claim, the money remains reserved. The five-minute response countdown does not begin until the first winning claim is submitted.
